Aurora is a web app that helps students at IME-USP plan their degree semester by semester. You lay out which courses you'll take and when, pick electives, and see how many credits are still missing before you can graduate.

It was built by a group of USP students, most of us from the Computer Science program (BCC), in partnership with IME-USP's Apoio Institucional. It's live at [aurora.ime.usp.br](https://aurora.ime.usp.br/). The interface is in Portuguese.
