This is the site you're on. I wanted something fun and simple where I could put my blog posts and projects, and it's still under construction.

## How it's built

It's a static site made with [Astro](https://astro.build/) and Tailwind, based on the [Astro Nano](https://github.com/markhorn-dev/astro-nano) template. Posts and project pages are plain Markdown files. Every push to `main` builds the site and deploys it to GitHub Pages.
